# What's new in Pixel Fighter

The notable changes to the game, newest first, one section per day (US Eastern time). Small fixes and tweaks are left out.

## Thursday, October 22, 2026

*Opening night: a giant roster of brawlers, dozens of stages and a lot of noise.*

- **Pixel Fighter is here.** A 16-bit style fighting game where every pixel, sound and note of music is made in code. Pick from Abe Lincoln, Nikola Tesla, Joan of Arc and Blackbeard and battle a CPU on three difficulty levels, or a friend.
- **Famous faces join the fight.** Chuck Norris, Bruce Lee, Muhammad Ali, Michael Jackson, Bob Ross, Stephen Hawking, Mother Teresa, King Tut, Oprah Winfrey, Gordon Ramsay, Guy Fieri, David Blaine, Steve Irwin, Shaquille O'Neal and Miss Cleo are all playable, each with special moves and a finishing move of their own. Yes, Oprah gives everyone a car.
- **Brand new characters too.** Meet the operatic cash man J.G. Spentworth, relic hunter Laura Kroft, Allison Wonderland, the vain supermodel Dorian Glare, Colonel Crisp, the overgrown kid Buster Giggleby, the kicks-only roller derby star Legs McGee, and Pamela Handerson, a giant hand that walks on two fingers.
- **Over twenty new stages.** Fight on a speeding night train, a neon skyscraper, a ghost ship, the low gravity Moon, a mushroom forest, a blizzard pass, a pyramid tomb, a beach, a cemetery, a cathedral, a rock concert, a wrestling ring, a dojo, a fashion runway and many more. Each has its own music and its own deadly stage finish.
- **Stages you can play with.** Shoot a basketball on The Hardwood, knock food off the shelves in Thriftopia Mart, and send balls flying in the burger joint's ball pit.
- **A party on the title screen.** The whole roster dances across the title screen in an endless conga line to a new original theme song, with a slim new menu that splits into Play and Options.
- **New character and stage select.** Fighters show up as little diorama tiles in a scrolling grid, you can pick your CPU opponent yourself, and after that you choose where to fight or let RANDOM decide.
- **Every fighter talks.** Each fighter makes a dramatic entrance before round one, and everyone now speaks in their own distinct voice. Press any button to skip.
- **Victory screen.** After each match, see a painted portrait of the winner standing over the beaten loser, along with a winning quote.
- **Smoother moves, easier controls.** Fighters animate more smoothly, the sweep, roundhouse and uppercut each got their own look, special moves are easier to pull off, and some specials now let you mash buttons for extra hits. A beaten opponent stays dazed for ten seconds so you can line up a finisher.
- **Bigger hits.** Punches, kicks and blocks got punchier layered sounds, every special move has its own sound effects, and hits throw starbursts and splatter that pools on the floor.
- **A secret cook.** Land a clean uppercut and, once in a while, a tiny cook may pop up in the corner with a surprise.
